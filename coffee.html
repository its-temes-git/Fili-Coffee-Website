Light Roast · Yirgacheffe

The Floral

Grown at 1,700–2,200m above sea level in the lush forests of southern Ethiopia, Yirgacheffe beans are celebrated worldwide for their exceptional clarity and complexity.

Expect bright citrus notes — think lemon zest and bergamot — woven with jasmine florals and a clean, tea-like finish. It's coffee as nature intended.

Region Yirgacheffe, SNNPR
Altitude 1,700–2,200m
Process Washed

Fan Favourite · Medium Roast · Sidama

The Bold

Sidama's volcanic red soil and high altitude create beans with an unusually complex cup profile. Each harvest brings forward the richness of dark chocolate, the brightness of wild blueberry, and a wine-like finish that lingers.

Medium roasted to balance sweetness and acidity, this is our most ordered coffee — and for good reason. It's accessible yet complex, familiar yet surprising.

Region Sidama Zone, SNNPR
Altitude 1,500–2,000m
Process Natural
Roast Medium

Dark Roast · Harrar

The Wild

From the ancient walled city of Harar in eastern Ethiopia, these beans are dry-processed and sun-dried on raised beds — a method unchanged for centuries.

The result is a coffee of wild intensity: exotic fruit, spice, and an almost wine-like fermentation quality. Dark roasted for a rich, velvety body. For those who want coffee bold and unapologetic.

Region Harrar, Oromia
Altitude 1,400–2,000m
Process Natural Dry
